How to update SMATRS

This is a temporary solution, as an updater will be done in the near future.

Note: You can start fresh from Chapter 2 from a new file. This is specifically for those that wish to keep their existing progress.

1. Go to your SMBX2 + SMATRS Folder.

2. Then open the data folder and locate the worlds folder.

3. Inside worlds, open your SMATRS Demo 2 folder.

4. Locate the following files: progress.json, .sav, sav#-ext.dat. (The # stands for a number) Copy these files and paste them somewhere on your computer where you can easily find them.

5. Now, go to the website and click "Download All". Save this file in a different location from your old SMBX2 + SMATRS folder.

6. Once downloaded, extract the ZIP.

7. Now, go into your newly downloaded SMBX2 + SMATRS folder, then data, then worlds.

8. Inside the SMATRS Chapter 1+2 folder, paste the progress and save files from earlier into this.

9. You have now carried over your files! You may delete your old SMBX2 + SMATRS folder if you wish.

10. Open the new SMATRS Launcher and your Save File(s) should show up!
